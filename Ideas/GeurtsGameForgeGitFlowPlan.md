## Implemented inspection milestone

The Editor-only no-code viewer and documented API share one lifecycle-owned read-only inspection service, God theme/menu integration and the supported inspect_queue operation. Explicit bounded refresh displays immutable queue/control observations, recorded source/size/version/commit, matching receipts, raw validation claims, local processor observations, lease states, failures and refresh timestamps. It never submits, starts a processor, acquires a lock, retries or saves configuration. Only public HTTPS and absolute local remotes are supported.

Eight owning disposable-Git/Editor fixtures passed on Unity 6000.6.3f1, zero failures/skips. They exercise preservation, faults, receipts, installed operation discovery/dispatch, UI/API result identity, HTTPS helper cancellation and cleanup. Native rendered layout/docking/skins/scaling and transient window visibility remain unverified. PID presence cannot certify ownership; lease audit dates cannot prove expiry; validation claims and current-main ancestry are not revalidated by the viewer. Refer to the exact owner release for limits and evidence. No game processor, runner or live consumer is changed.

## Implemented generic publisher milestone

The generic Git adapter adds shared no-code/API policy review/save, immutable submit-once handoff and a separately extracted independent Windows/.NET host. Its committed project policy declares a plain SemVer file, protected controllers, pinned console tools/dependencies and strictly required validation commands. Workers retain their original gf_<exact-source> ID and stop waiting; submission never launches a processor. God owns saved form drafts and mutation admission.

One configured root holds an exclusive local owner and a persistent native protocol-1 renewable lease in a separate namespace. Queue-owned versions, retained WIP/attempt evidence, all-or-nothing lease/main/result publication, exact fetched verification and post-publication pulls are implemented. The host renews independently during validation, observes compatible abandonment monotonically and permanently fences uncertain owners. Required checks, ownership/access failures and unknown dirty work remain blockers.

Finite explicit continuation supports an unchanged clean recorded candidate on its original integrated main/policy or finishes its original matching published receipt/pull. It preserves IDs, versions, receipts, failures and attempt bounds. Conflicted/dirty/changed candidates and runner migration remain blocked rather than reset. The generic direct-main adapter does not waive projects' PR/release/native metadata requirements; those need owning adapters. Exact source/environment evidence and unverified native presentation/access surfaces belong to the owning release. Consumer and runner adoption remain manual; Dreadvault's running tooling is untouched.
